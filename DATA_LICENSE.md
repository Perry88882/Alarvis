https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

appropriate credit, link to the license, and indicate whether changes were
made.

## Scope and exclusions

- The license covers the records and tables produced by the authors.
- It does not cover third-party program names or any third-party code; no
  third-party code is included.
- Model responses were generated by the respective model providers. The
  released form is reduced to a one-word verdict; no model reasoning or free
  text is redistributed. Users remain responsible for complying with the terms
  of the model providers when reusing the records.
- The license does not grant rights to the commercial API aggregation service
  or its marks; the service is not identified in this repository.
